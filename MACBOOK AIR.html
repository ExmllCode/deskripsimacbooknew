Key Advantages of MacBook Air

Completely Silent

Because Apple Silicon is so power efficient, MacBook Air delivers incredible sustained performance with zero fans. No matter the workload, it runs 100% silent.

Dual External Displays

MacBook Air with M3 supports up to two external displays simultaneously when the laptop lid is closed, making it ideal for dual-monitor workstation desks.

Four Stunning Finishes

Available in Midnight (with an advanced anodization layer to reduce fingerprints), Starlight, Space Gray, and classic Silver, each with a color-matched MagSafe cable.
