The Architecture Shift

The Revolution of Apple Silicon

In November 2020, Apple announced the M1 chip, marking the historic transition away from Intel processors toward custom in-house ARM architecture. By designing the chip, firmware, and macOS in unison, Apple achieved performance-per-watt ratios that revolutionized portable computing. With the M2, M3, and now M4 generations built on 3-nanometer processes, MacBook delivers workstation rendering speeds while sipping battery power.

MacBook Milestone Timeline

Two decades of relentless industrial design and engineering innovation.

2006 Original MacBook

Steve Jobs introduces the first Intel MacBook Pro, replacing the PowerBook G4 and pioneering the original magnetic MagSafe power adapter.

2008 The Manila Envelope

Steve Jobs pulls the original MacBook Air out of a manila envelope, redefining the ultraportable laptop category forever.

2012 Retina Display

MacBook Pro with Retina Display debuts with 220 pixels per inch, HDMI, flash storage, and a dramatically thinner unibody profile.

2020-2026 Apple Silicon

Transition to M1, M2, M3, and M4 chips. 24-hour battery, Liquid Retina XDR, ProMotion 120Hz, and unified memory up to 128GB.
